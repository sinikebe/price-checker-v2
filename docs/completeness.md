# Published catalogue completeness

The public **À compléter** page audits records that are already public. It never
publishes private product or price drafts. A published record remains visible in
the catalogue while it is being completed; withdrawal is a separate moderation
decision.

## Current rules

- **Product, mandatory:** name, brand, package, quantity. These are the same
  four fields checked before a new product draft may be published. Previously
  published records that lack one appear in the priority section.
- **Store, mandatory:** city (or served area) and postal code. New manual store
  submissions and Open Prices locations require both. Previously published
  stores with either field absent appear in the priority section. They are not
  auto-matched to bookmarklet captures or accepted for new price submissions
  until completed. An administrator can fill the missing fields on the page.
- **Product, optional enrichment:** barcode and composition. Missing values
  appear below the mandatory section only when the product has no mandatory
  gaps. Neither value blocks publication. Other optional fields are not
  currently scored; this policy can be refined without changing old records.

The report is calculated from current, non-withdrawn published products and
stores associated with public price observations. Imported or transferred
historical records are not silently dropped or rewritten. The page is
paginated, and counts are recalculated after a correction.
